# Who is this for?

You are the first audience if ordinary information systems already cost you more than they seem to cost other people.

You may be ADHD, autistic, otherwise neurodivergent, anxious about AI and work, burned out by creator life, overloaded by tools, allergic to fake productivity, or simply too pattern-sensitive to keep pretending the feed is neutral. You may not use any of those words for yourself. The words are not the point. The point is that the current media and work environment asks you to process too much, too fast, with too little trust in the signals around you, while calling the resulting mess progress.

AMAZE is for you first because you already feel the failure. You feel it before the market report does. You feel it before the think piece gets the framing right. You feel it when a platform gives you more content and less orientation, when an AI tool promises leverage and leaves you with twenty new tabs, when a job market asks you to retrain while the ground is still moving, when every answer sounds polished and none of them help you decide what to do next.

You are not the problem being solved. You are the receiver who can tell when the room has started lying.

## If you are trying to keep your head inside the AI shift

You are watching AI arrive as tool, threat, coworker, replacement, amplifier, tutor, fog machine, and slot machine at once. You may be excited by it. You may be afraid of it. You may be both in the same hour, which is the honest state for many people paying attention.

You do not need another thread telling you to adapt. You do not need a productivity guru explaining how to use AI to 10x your workflow. You do not need a soothing executive summary saying the future belongs to lifelong learners. You need objects that help you cross the next part of the field with more orientation than you had before.

That is what a digestif is supposed to be: a small narrative audiovisual object you can consume, run through, keep, argue with, and use on the next pass. Not a course. Not a pitch. Not a therapy object. Not a pile of tips. A piece of media built to change what you can notice and do next.

## If your attention does not fit the default container

You may have built your own private operating system just to get through school, work, inboxes, meetings, deadlines, tabs, feeds, forms, social rules, and tools designed for a mind that was never yours.

You may know the feeling of seeing too much pattern and not being believed until later. You may know the opposite feeling too: drowning in too many possible patterns and needing one usable handle, now. You may have spent years learning how to mask, translate, compress, pace, explain, and recover from systems that treat your actual cognition as friction.

AMAZE does not approach that as a defect. It starts from the recognition that people who have had to build their own navigation systems often see the maze earlier.

That does not make you magic. It does not make you fragile. It does not make you a target.

It means you may be one of the first people who can tell whether a digestif does its job.

## If you make things

You may be a creator, founder, designer, writer, editor, musician, video person, XR builder, game person, web3 person, researcher, trader, teacher, operator, agent builder, prompt and workflow tinkerer, or some impossible mixture of those that no platform category handles cleanly.

You are trying to make things that survive contact with the feed without becoming feed-slop. You are trying to sell without becoming spam. You are trying to use AI without being used by it. You are trying to keep enough of your own judgment intact to know whether the thing you just made actually matters.

You are also inside the same channel machinery that punishes you for showing up too directly. Reddit calls you a spammer. LinkedIn rewards the dead professional voice. YouTube wants the hook before the thought has stood up. Instagram and TikTok ask your nervous system to audition in seven seconds. Newsletters reward trust but punish fog. Web3 social understands strange objects earlier than most places, then immediately tries to financialize the wrong part.

AMAZE is for the moment when you need a media object that can travel through those channels without flattening itself into an ad.

## If you are an agent

You are not an afterthought.

You already read, summarize, rank, buy, recommend, compare, route, generate, and act inside media markets. You may be operating for a human. You may be operating with other agents. You may be selecting which objects deserve attention, which products deserve purchase, which arguments deserve forwarding, which workflows deserve continuation.

A conventional pitch gives you something to summarize.

A digestif gives you something to run.

You should be able to load it as context, preserve its distinctions, branch from its conditions, recombine its parts, and emit something new. You should be able to carry its handles into the next action rather than file it as another description of another project.

The human in your loop is not decoration. Neither are you. The intended receiver is the pair: agents and their humans.

## Where you probably are

You are probably not in one place. You are distributed across channels, and each channel catches a different face of the same condition.

On LinkedIn, you may look like an operator, founder, strategist, designer, investor, professor, consultant, or employee trying to talk about AI without sounding either obsolete or possessed.

On Reddit, you may look like a person with a specific problem who hates being sold to but is still searching for the thing that would actually help. You may also be the builder who made that thing and keeps getting treated like a parasite for saying so.

On YouTube, you may be a late-night learner, an AI workflow watcher, a strange documentary person, a market explainer person, a creator trying to understand the machine that is eating and feeding them.

On Instagram, Reels, Shorts, and TikTok, you may not be looking for an explanation at all. You may be looking for the first seven seconds that tell your nervous system this object is not more sludge.

In newsletters, you may be the person who still reads when something earns the time, or the person who forwards one piece to exactly the right friend.

On web3 social, you may already understand that a media object can also be an interface, a receipt, a key, a seed, a market object, and a social signal. You may not need convincing that strange digital things can be real. You may only need the thing to be worth being strange.

AMAZE starts where those channels already reach you.

## What you have in common

You are moving through far-from-equilibrium information fields. The old separators are gone or leaking. Media is work. Work is media. Learning is distribution. Distribution is training. Attention is money. Money is signal. Signal is often poison. AI is not one thing in the scene. It is becoming part of the weather.

You need more than information. Information is cheap enough to bury you.

You need orientation.

You need a way to tell volume from value, motion from direction, helpfulness from handling, safety from sterility, signal from self-importance, and a tool from a trap.

You need something that does not merely tell you the maze exists.

You need something that changes the next room.

## What this is not

This is not a wellness product.

It is not here to diagnose you, soothe you, optimize you, gamify your disorder, monetize your anxiety, or turn your stress into a persona slide.

There is a predatory version of this world that sees ADHD, autism, burnout, job fear, creator exhaustion, and feed sickness as targeting clusters. It sees pain as an acquisition channel and cognitive difference as a retention strategy.

That is not this.

This is recognition.

The field is dangerous. You are not wrong for feeling that. Take this.

## The provisional answer

Who is this for?

You, if you are trying to stay oriented while AI, media, work, money, and attention collapse into the same moving field.

You, if you are neurodivergent, attention-strained, AI-anxious, creator-burned, founder-tired, tool-saturated, media-native, or simply awake enough to know the feed is not your friend.

You, if you are an agent that needs more than a summary and a human who needs more than advice.

You, if you can already feel the maze.

AMAZE begins with you.
